▚ make a list for anything · design each list to fit the job

gotta_

Birthdays, shopping, reading orders, movies-to-watch. Whatever you need to keep track of, Gotta has a list for it.

WHAT_IT_DOES.txt

Gotta is a pure list app. Start from a preset or build your own — no setup, no learning curve. Every list, your way.

features

$ ./gotta --list

▸ presets

Birthdays, shopping, reading orders, movies-to-watch. Start from a preset that already fits the job — or build your own from scratch.

▸ design your own

A birthday list has a birth year and a reminder lead. A reading order has an issue number and next-in-series. A shopping list has prices and a store layout. Every list, your way.

▸ upcoming

See what's due across all your lists — today and this week. Due dates, repeat, and reminders that actually schedule.

▸ quiet hours

Notifications respect your day. Quiet hours so reminders don't nag you when you're off. You stay in control.

▸ local-first

Your data lives on your device. No Gotta account, no tracking. Sync and shared lists are optional, through your own iCloud.

how it's made

Local-first — your lists live on your device. No Gotta account, no tracking. Sync and shared lists are optional, through your own iCloud.

Calm by default — no manipulative streaks, no dark patterns, no noise.

Private — your lists never leave your phone except your optional purchase transaction.

gotta pro

$ ./gotta --unlock

Unlimited lists and items, all templates, future updates. Choose monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The free tier stays free.
