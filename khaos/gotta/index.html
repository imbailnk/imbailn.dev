Gotta — your notebook, superpowered.

One calm place for the stuff you want to keep up with — and the days you want to remember.

What it does

  • Trackers — lists for anything: groceries, books to read, birthdays, party planning, wishlists. Prices, quantities, dates, reminders — whatever each list needs.
  • Habits — build consistency with a calm monthly journal. No guilt streaks; just a record of your days.
  • Journal — a day-by-day timeline of what you did, what you logged, and what you wrote. Backfill past days freely.
  • Time — log what you spent your day on, simply. Labels and minutes, no timers fighting for your attention.
  • Reminders — optional, on your schedule. Quiet hours respected. You stay in control.

The way it's built

  • Local-first — everything lives on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Export your data anytime as JSON.
  • Calm by default — no manipulative streaks, no dark patterns, no noise.
  • Private — no data leaves your phone except your optional purchase transaction.
