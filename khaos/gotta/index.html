gotta_

One calm place for the stuff you want to keep up with — and the days you want to remember.

WHAT_IT_DOES.txt

Gotta is a notebook, superpowered. Lists, habits, time and memories — one calm place. No account, no noise.

features

$ ./gotta --list

▸ trackers

Lists for anything: groceries, books to read, birthdays, party planning, wishlists. Prices, quantities, dates, reminders — whatever each list needs.

▸ habits

Build consistency with a calm monthly journal. No guilt streaks; just a record of your days.

▸ journal

A day-by-day timeline of what you did, what you logged, and what you wrote. Backfill past days freely.

▸ time

Log what you spent your day on, simply. Labels and minutes, no timers fighting for your attention.

▸ reminders

Optional, on your schedule. Quiet hours respected. You stay in control.

the way it's built

$ ./gotta --philosophy

local-first calm by default private no account export anytime
build notes

how it's made

Local-first — everything lives on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Export your data anytime as JSON.

Calm by default — no manipulative streaks, no dark patterns, no noise.

Private — no data leaves your phone except your optional purchase transaction.
