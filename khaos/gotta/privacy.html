privacy policy

gotta · KHA OS LLC · effective: 2026-10-26

Publisher: KHA OS LLC, a New York limited liability company
Contact: support@imbailn.dev

1 · summary

Gotta is designed so your data stays on your device. Gotta does not create an account, does not sync your data to a server, and does not sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter into Gotta — your lists and their items — is stored locally on your device only.

2 · information we do not collect

  • No account. You do not register, create a profile, or provide a name or email to use Gotta.
  • No cloud storage. Your lists, items, and settings never leave your device.
  • No advertising and no tracking of your content. Gotta contains no advertising SDKs and we do not build a profile of you. Your list contents are never transmitted.

3 · crash diagnostics (sentry)

To keep Gotta reliable, it uses Sentry to collect anonymous crash diagnostics when the app crashes:

  • Sentry receives crash reports (a stack trace, device model, OS version, and app version) for the sole purpose of fixing bugs.
  • It is configured to not collect personal data: no user identifiers, no list contents, no message bodies (sendDefaultPii is off), and no interaction/UI-label tracing.
  • Crash data is transmitted to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) as a third-party processor and is not used for advertising or profiling.

4 · purchases

At launch Gotta is free — there are no purchases or subscriptions. If Gotta Pro (paid features) is added in a future version, purchases will be processed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; payment details would be handled entirely by Apple or Google, never by KHA OS LLC, and this policy would be updated before that version ships.

5 · device permissions

Gotta may request notification permission so it can remind you about things you set (for example, birthdays or due dates). Notifications are generated on your device; enabling them is optional and you can turn them off at any time in Gotta's settings or your device settings.

6 · children's privacy

Gotta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7 · data retention & deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control it completely:

  • Export: Gotta can export your data as a file from Settings.
  • Delete: you can delete individual items or clear all data from Settings, or simply delete the app.

We hold no copies of your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8 · changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected before the affected version is released.
